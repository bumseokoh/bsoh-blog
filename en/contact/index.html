What I'm glad to hear about

  • Corrections — if a route, distance, or facility detail doesn't match reality, tell me. I'll verify it, fix the post, and note the correction.
  • Copyright or image questions — if you believe a photo or quotation is a problem, send the URL of the post.
  • Comment removal or edits — send the post URL and the comment text and I'll take care of it.
  • Privacy requests — see the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
  • Questions about gear or routes, or just saying hello — always welcome.

What I politely decline

I don't reply to paid link placements, requests to insert advertising links into existing posts, or bulk affiliate pitches. This blog is meant to be a record of things I actually bought, used, and walked.
